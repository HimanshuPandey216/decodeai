About Decode AI

"Complex technical knowledge should be easier to understand, discover, and share."

What is Decode AI?

Decode AI is an open community-driven learning platform founded by Himanshu Pandey. It brings together engineers, researchers, students, and practitioners to share high-quality study materials, paper explainers, book summaries, and career roadmaps.

The platform is not about hoarding video courses. It is about Learning, Understanding, Building, and Sharing practical knowledge.

How Decode AI Works

1. Learn Reliable Sources

Study seminal papers, university courses, and textbooks.

2. Simplify Concepts

Deconstruct dense equations and algorithms into intuitive guides.

3. Create Useful Notes

Format notes with visual diagrams and clean code blocks.

4. Share & Help Others

Publish openly so the next learner can decode the topic faster.
