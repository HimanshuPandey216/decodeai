📤 How to Upload Your AI HTML Notes

Got your own HTML study notes, cheat sheets, or AI guides? Uploading them to Decode AI is simple:

Step 1
Place your .html note file inside content/html/ in your repo.
Step 2
Add a 5-line JSON entry inside data/ai-concepts.json with your note title & file link.
Step 3
Push to GitHub! Vercel will auto-deploy your new AI concept note instantly.
