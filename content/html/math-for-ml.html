Mathematics for Machine Learning Essentials

1. Linear Algebra Fundamentals

Matrices transform space. Key concepts:

  • Matrix Multiplication: Non-commutative transformation of linear bases ($A B \neq B A$).
  • Eigenvectors ($v$) & Eigenvalues ($\lambda$): Vectors whose direction remains unchanged during a linear transformation: $A v = \lambda v$.

2. Multivariate Calculus & Optimization

  • Gradient Vector ($\nabla f$): Vector of partial derivatives pointing in the direction of steepest ascent.
  • Jacobian Matrix: Matrix of first-order partial derivatives of a vector-valued function.
  • Hessian Matrix: Matrix of second-order partial derivatives used to verify local minima, maxima, or saddle points.
