Phase 0 · Orientation

Understanding the ML Engineer Role

Before writing a line of code, know what job you're actually training for. "Machine Learning Engineer" sits at the intersection of software engineering, applied statistics and production systems.

What an ML Engineer actually does

In one lineTakes a trained model (or trains one) and turns it into a reliable, monitored, scalable piece of production software — the engineering around the model, not just the model itself.
Day to dayWriting data pipelines, training and retraining models, writing evaluation scripts, wrapping models in APIs, debugging why a metric dropped in production.

ML Engineer vs. other roles

RoleCore focusTypical output
ML EngineerBuilding, training, deploying and maintaining ML models as production systemsAPIs, pipelines, deployed models, monitoring dashboards
Data ScientistExploratory analysis, statistical modeling, generating business insightNotebooks, reports, dashboards, prototype models
AI EngineerBuilding products on top of large pretrained models (mostly LLMs) via APIsRAG pipelines, agents, prompt systems
Data EngineerBuilding and maintaining the pipelines that move and store data at scaleETL/ELT pipelines, data warehouses
