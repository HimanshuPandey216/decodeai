RESEARCH EXPLAINER

Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017)

Original Authors: Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, Illia Polosukhin.

1. Executive Summary

The landmark paper proposing the Transformer, the first sequence transduction model based entirely on self-attention mechanisms without relying on sequence-aligned RNNs or convolution.

2. Key Innovations

  • Multi-Head Attention: Allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions.
  • Residual Connections & LayerNorm: $LayerNorm(x + SubLayer(x))$ ensures stable gradient propagation in deep stacks (6 encoder & 6 decoder layers).
  • Sinusoidal Positional Encoding: Injects relative position information using $PE_{(pos, 2i)} = \sin(pos / 10000^{2i/d_{model}})$.
